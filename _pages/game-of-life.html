How it works ▼

Survive: the number of neighbours that allow a live cell to survive to the next generation.

Born: the number of neighbours that cause a dead cell to become alive.

Conway's Game of Life uses {"survive": [2, 3], "born": [3]}:

  • A live cell survives with 2 or 3 neighbours.
  • A dead cell becomes alive with exactly 3 neighbours.

Click cells on the grid to toggle them, then press Start.
